E-Pharmacy is an online platform for ordering medicines and health products. The service simplifies the process of searching and purchasing medicines, allowing users to order the necessary drugs right from home.
Main features:
Online catalog with convenient search and filtering by category, brand and price.
Detailed product cards with description, instructions and price.
Cart and checkout with a step-by-step process.
Authorization and registration system for saving order history.
Adaptive design, convenient for mobile devices.
Pages:
Home — welcome screen with categories and promotional offers.
Catalog — full list of products with filtering.
List of pharmacies — where you can buy drugs, and their opening hours.
Product card — detailed information and the "Add to cart" button.
Cart — viewing and editing orders before payment.
Authorization / Registration — login and account creation.
